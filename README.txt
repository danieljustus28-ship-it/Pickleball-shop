THE PICKLEBALL SHOP — LAUNCH INSTRUCTIONS

1. Open index.html to preview your site in a browser.
2. Sign up at https://github.com and create a PUBLIC repository named pickleball-shop.
3. Choose Add file > Upload files, upload index.html, then commit changes.
4. In the repository choose Settings > Pages > Build and deployment > Deploy from a branch; select main and /(root), then Save.
5. GitHub will display the public website URL, usually https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io/pickleball-shop/ . Publishing can take a few minutes.
6. Review the website on a phone and computer. Replace the placeholder business contact sentence before promotion.
7. Apply for Amazon Associates at https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/ using your published website URL. Follow the current approval and qualifying sales rules.
8. After enrolling, replace the ordinary Amazon search links in index.html with your own Amazon-approved Special Links generated in Associates SiteStripe or another approved tool. Do not just invent an affiliate tag. For each category, replace the link's href with your approved URL; update the 'no commission link yet' label and the pre-enrollment disclosure text when appropriate.
9. Only use Amazon product images, product data, prices and ratings through authorized Amazon tools under the applicable terms. The starter website intentionally uses emojis instead of scraped Amazon images.
10. Do not claim this is an Amazon-owned shop. Customers buy directly on Amazon. Keep buying guides useful and original, check links regularly, and follow applicable privacy, FTC and affiliate policies.
