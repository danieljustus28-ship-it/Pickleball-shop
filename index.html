Quick buying guides

🏓 Your first paddle

For a first paddle, prioritize a comfortable grip, forgiving sweet spot and manageable weight. Avoid choosing solely by power claims.

🎯 Control vs. power

Control-oriented paddles can make resets and soft shots easier to manage; power-oriented designs may reward a faster swing but require touch.

🟡 Outdoor balls

Check whether balls are labeled indoor or outdoor. Outdoor balls are usually designed to handle wind and rougher playing surfaces.

👟 Court shoes

Look for lateral support and traction suitable for your court. Running shoes are designed mainly for forward movement.
